Parents' Paperwork

A shared app for adult children, often living abroad, to run their retired parents' yearly paperwork in India: pension life certificates, tax declarations, deposits, insurance, documents and government benefits, in one family calendar with step-by-step guides.

Try it

  1. On Today, open Dad's life certificate and tick off the walkthrough steps.
  2. Change who owns the task, then mark it done.
  3. Open Timeline to see the whole tax year, April to March.
  4. Check Docs for missing papers and Trackers for deposits and nominees.
  5. Open Government benefits to see what Mom and Dad can claim.
