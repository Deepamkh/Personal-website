PORTFOLIO CASE STUDY

CryptoSense AI

An AI-powered sentiment analysis concept that turns social, news and on-chain signal into a single investment-likelihood score per cryptocurrency - with a live, mobile-first prototype below.

View the prototype Open fullscreen Read the system design doc

The problem

Retail crypto investors track a lot of scattered signal with no single place that turns it into a consistent, comparable score across assets.

Social chatter, news coverage, on-chain flows and raw price action each tell part of the story - but reading all four for every asset you're watching doesn't scale. CryptoSense AI collapses them into one composite score, ranks every tracked currency by it, and keeps the reasoning visible rather than hiding it behind a single number.

  • A single, comparable investment-likelihood score (0–100%) per currency
  • Visible reasoning - which sources drove the score, and why
  • A ranked list so opportunities are easy to scan
  • A 7-day trend forecast shown as a range, not a false-precision number

Live prototype

Interactive - tap a currency to open its detail view. Data is simulated for this demo; see the disclaimer inside the app.

Scoring methodology

Each currency gets three 0–100 sub-scores, blended into one composite score and mapped to a plain-language label.

SignalWeightWhat feeds it
Social sentiment40%Volume-weighted NLP sentiment across X, Reddit, Telegram, with bot/spam filtering applied first
News sentiment35%NLP sentiment and relevance scoring over licensed news APIs and RSS feeds
On-chain / market signal25%Wallet accumulation, exchange in/outflows, volatility, liquidity depth

A hype-vs-substance penalty is subtracted when social sentiment sharply outpaces news and on-chain signal - a proxy for speculative activity with limited underlying support - before mapping to the final label:

Score rangeLabelInterpretation
75–100Strong buy signalBroad, consistent positive signal across all three sources
60–74Buy signalNet positive signal, some mixed inputs
40–59Hold / neutralSignals roughly balanced or conflicting
25–39Caution advisedNet negative signal, elevated uncertainty
0–24Avoid signalBroadly negative signal across sources

System architecture

End-to-end flow from raw source data to the mobile client.

  • Data sources: social platform APIs (X, Reddit, Telegram), licensed news APIs/RSS, blockchain nodes and indexers, exchange market-data feeds
  • Ingestion layer: stream collectors for high-frequency sources and scheduled batch collectors for news/on-chain snapshots, buffered through a message queue into a raw data lake
  • NLP sentiment pipeline: transformer-based classifiers (FinBERT-style) with entity linking, topic tagging, and bot/spam filtering
  • Forecasting models: per-asset time-series models producing a 7-day expected path with confidence bands, retrained on a rolling schedule
  • Scoring engine: weighted composite formula, hype-penalty adjustment, versioned score history for auditability
  • API layer: REST/GraphQL service exposing rankings, per-asset detail, and historical/forecast series
  • Mobile app: React Native client rendering the rankings and detail screens shown above
